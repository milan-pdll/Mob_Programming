Mob Programming

Mob programming is a software development approach where the whole team works on the same code, at the same time, in the same space, and on the same computer. It is an extension of pair programming, where instead of two developers working together, the entire team collaborates on a single task.

In mob programming, the team typically consists of a driver, who is responsible for typing the code, and navigators, who provide guidance, suggestions, and feedback. The team rotates roles frequently to ensure that everyone has a chance to contribute and learn from each other.
