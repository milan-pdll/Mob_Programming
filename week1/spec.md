Project Proposal: Cultural Services Platform
Sanskriti — A Digital Platform for Cultural Services and Practitioners

1. Problem Statement
Nepal is a culturally diverse country with different communities following their own traditions, rituals, and cultural practices. People often need specific cultural practitioners such as Pandits, Purohits, Lamas, priests, or traditional performers for ceremonies and cultural events. However, finding the appropriate practitioner is often dependent on personal contacts and word of mouth. There is a need for a centralized platform where people can easily discover and connect with suitable cultural practitioners based on their ceremony, tradition, location, and requirements.
2. Objectives
•	To provide a centralized platform for discovering cultural practitioners and traditional service providers.
•	To help users find suitable practitioners based on ceremony, cultural tradition, and location.
•	To allow practitioners to create profiles and showcase the services they provide.
•	To simplify communication, service requests, and future booking between users and practitioners.
•	To support the accessibility and preservation of Nepal's diverse cultural traditions.
3. Proposed Solution
Sanskriti will connect people who need cultural services with relevant and verified practitioners. Users will be able to search for services such as Puja, Bratabandha, weddings, Buddhist ceremonies, and other cultural practices. Practitioners can create profiles, list their services, and respond to service requests.
4. Core Features
•	User and practitioner registration
•	Search and filter cultural services
•	Practitioner profiles and service information
•	Location and tradition-based discovery
•	Service requests and booking
•	Ratings and reviews
5. Conclusion
Sanskriti aims to make cultural services easier to find and access while providing cultural practitioners with a digital platform to reach people who need their services. The long-term goal is to contribute to the accessibility and preservation of Nepal's diverse cultural practices.
